## What is NOT stock

`public/assets/*.mp4` and the four `*-poster.jpg` frames that go with them are
SmartFilm's own product footage. No stock library has a clip of PDLC glass
switching from opaque to clear, which is the one thing the product does, so the
films stayed and only the photography around them was replaced.
